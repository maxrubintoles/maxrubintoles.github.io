About

I’m a second-year (G2) PhD student in Computer Science at Harvard SEAS, where I’m very fortunate to be advised by Ariel Procaccia. My research interests lie at the intersection of AI and economics.

I’m currently supported by an NSF Graduate Research Fellowship (NSF GRFP).

Previously, I graduated summa cum laude from the University of Pennsylvania, where I received the Computer Science Award, given to one student per graduating class. At Penn, I worked with Surbhi Goel and Aaron Roth.
